# Privacy Policy for IssueBuddyTools

This privacy policy explains how IssueBuddyTools handles user data. We value your privacy and keep data collection to an absolute minimum.

## 1. What Data We Receive and How
We do not personally or directly collect any personal data through the software. 

* **How data is received:** Upon download, your contact information is securely shared with us by the Autodesk App Store.
* **Data received:** This includes only your contact name and email address.

## 2. How We Use and Retain Your Data
* **Usage:** We use your email address strictly to send critical software updates and notifications regarding IssueBuddyTools. 
* **Data Sharing:** We do not lease, sell, or distribute your information. Any third-party platform we utilize to maintain your notifications is strictly required to provide the exact same level of data protection as stated in this policy.
* **Retention:** We only retain your email address for as long as you use the software or wish to receive updates.

## 3. How to Revoke Consent and Request Deletion
You have the right to opt-out or have your data purged at any time. You can explicitly **revoke your consent** or **request full deletion** of your data by contacting us directly at **davidveld@gmail.com**. Once requested, your records will be permanently deleted from our systems.
